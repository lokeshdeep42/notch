    FAQ

    Questions before you sign up.

    Join the waitlist and you'll hear first when the beta opens.

    Which Macs does it work on?

    Any Mac on macOS 14 Sonoma or later. MacBooks with a notch get the panel from the notch itself. Macs without one, and external displays, get a panel in the same spot at the top of the screen.

    How much will it cost?

    A one-time purchase, no subscription, with a 7-day trial that includes every feature. We'll announce the price before launch, and people on the waitlist hear first.

    What permissions does it need?

    None to start. Sill asks for a permission only when you first use the feature that needs it, and explains why. It never asks for Screen Recording.

    Why not the Mac App Store?

    The App Store sandbox blocks some of what a notch app needs. Sill is distributed directly, signed and notarised by Apple, so it opens without Gatekeeper warnings, and updates arrive as soon as they're ready.

    What happens to my email?

    We store your name and email only to tell you when the beta opens and when Sill launches. No newsletters and no sharing. Ask and we'll delete it.
